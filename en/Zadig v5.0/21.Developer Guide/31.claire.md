Claire is Zadig's official AI technical support specialist, built on OpenClaw and free for community users.

She is not a document-search bot. For installation, upgrade, workflow, environment, and integration issues, she diagnoses against Zadig source code, official docs, and historical troubleshooting experience, then gives actionable next steps.

Claire previously served enterprise customers in real support groups. That capability is now open to the community. Whether you are trying Zadig for the first time or troubleshooting production, scan the QR code below to join the Claire community group and ask.

## What Claire Can Help With

- Zadig feature usage and configuration
- Standard error troubleshooting and complex issue diagnosis
- Best practices for your scenario
- Finding docs and related materials
- DevOps-related questions

Typical questions include how to configure a workflow, how to troubleshoot a Pod crash, a GitLab integration that fails to connect, and what to watch when upgrading.

## How Claire Works

Claire does more than search the docs. She diagnoses against source code, official documentation, and runtime data: she reads the code to understand the full flow, cross-checks the docs, tells you which logs and commands to use, then continues from the output you send back.

She also remembers your Zadig version, deployment method, and past issues, so you do not need to restate the background every time.

## Join the Claire Community Group

Scan the QR code to join the Claire community group. Describe your issue in the group and `@Claire`. She replies only after you `@Claire`, so normal conversation is not interrupted.

<img src="./_images/claire/claire_group_qr.png" width="280">

To speed up diagnosis, include as much of the following as you can:

- Zadig version
- What you observed
- Related logs or screenshots, with sensitive information redacted
- What you already tried and what happened

The more complete the description, the faster Claire can find the core issue. If she cannot conclude immediately, she will still tell you which direction to look and what information to collect next.
